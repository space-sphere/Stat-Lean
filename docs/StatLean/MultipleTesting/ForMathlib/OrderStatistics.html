    Order statistics of a finite real tuple — ForMathlib layer #

    Theorem-agnostic bricks (pure math, Mathlib-only) used by the Benjamini–Hochberg and Holm assembly files: the order statistics v₍₀₎ ≤ v₍₁₎ ≤ ⋯ of a tuple v : Fin n → ℝ, built from Mathlib's Tuple.sort (the permutation that sorts v).

    orderStat v i = v (Tuple.sort v i) is the i-th smallest entry; orderStat_monotone records that i ↦ orderStat v i is monotone. These let the assembly files write the BH cutoff p₍ᵢ₎ ≤ iα/N and the Holm step-down cutoffs against sorted p-values.
