    Bounds on local polynomial weights #

    Under the standing assumptions (eigenvalue lower bound, design density bound, boxed kernel) and h ≥ 1/(2n), the LP(ℓ) weights satisfy, uniformly over t ∈ [0,1]:

    • |W*ᵢ(t)| ≤ C*/(n·h);
    • ∑ i, |W*ᵢ(t)| ≤ C*;
    • W*ᵢ(t) = 0 whenever |xdat i − t| > h,

    with the explicit constant C* = lpWeightConst Kmax lam0 a₀ = max{2K_max/λ₀, 4K_max·a₀/λ₀}.

    Proof formalization notes. (iii) is immediate from the kernel's support. For (i): |W*ᵢ| ≤ (nh)⁻¹·‖B⁻¹U(zᵢ)K(zᵢ)‖ ≤ (nh)⁻¹·K_max·‖U(zᵢ)‖/λ₀ by the inverse bound (lpMatrix_inv_mulVec_sq_le); on |z| ≤ 1, ‖U(z)‖² = ∑ (z^k/k!)² ≤ ∑ 1/k! ≤ e ≤ 4 (lpBasis_normSq_le), so ‖U(zᵢ)‖ ≤ 2. For (ii), sum (i) over the at most n·a₀·max(2h, 1/n) = 2a₀·n·h indices with |xᵢ − t| ≤ h (design density bound, using h ≥ 1/(2n) to resolve the max).

    Bibliographic comments. These weight bounds are the standard technical core of local polynomial risk analysis; cf. C. J. Stone, Ann. Statist. 8 (1980), 1348–1360, and J. Fan and I. Gijbels, Local Polynomial Modelling and Its Applications (1996).
