    Information-loss operator (observed-data score via conditional expectation) #

    In a coarsening (missing-data) model one observes not the full datum but a coarsened summary, recorded by a measurement map $M : \Omega_{\mathrm{full}} \to \Omega_{\mathrm{obs}}$. The full-data model induces the observed-data distribution $P_{\mathrm{obs}} = P_{\mathrm{full}} \circ M^{-1}$ (the pushforward of $P_{\mathrm{full}}$ under $M$). The information-loss operator sends a full-data score $g$ to the observed-data score obtained by taking the conditional expectation of $g$ given the observed data, i.e. given the sub-$\sigma$-algebra $M^{-1}(\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})$ generated by $M$: $$ g \;\longmapsto\; E_{P_{\mathrm{full}}}\!\bigl[\, g \mid M^{-1} (\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})\,\bigr]. $$ Because this conditional expectation is $M^{-1}(\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})$-measurable, the Doob–Dynkin factorization identifies it with a genuine function of the observed data, giving a bounded linear map between the mean-zero square-integrable spaces $L^2_0(P_{\mathrm{full}}) \to L^2_0(P_{\mathrm{obs}})$.

    In Lean this is realized via condExpL2 (the orthogonal projection onto the $L^2$ subspace of $M^{-1}(\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})$-measurable functions) composed with the Doob $L^2$ isomorphism doobL2Equiv, which transports an $M^{-1}(\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})$-measurable element of $L^2(P_{\mathrm{full}})$ to the corresponding element of $L^2(P_{\mathrm{obs}})$. The two measures involved are $P_{\mathrm{full}}$ on $\Omega_{\mathrm{full}}$ and $P_{\mathrm{obs}} = P_{\mathrm{full}}.\mathrm{map}\,M$ on $\Omega_{\mathrm{obs}}$.

    Reference. A. W. van der Vaart, Asymptotic Statistics, Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics, Cambridge University Press, 1998, Chapter 25 (Semiparametric Models), §25.5 (Score and Information Operators), the Information Loss Models subsection (§25.5.2), with related material in §25.6 (Missing and Coarsening at Random), Lemma 25.34.

    Proof formalization notes. The information-loss operator bridges ↥(L²₀(P_full)) →L[ℝ] ↥(L²₀(P_obs)) via condExpL2 (conditional expectation as an orthogonal $L^2$ projection onto $M^{-1}(\Sigma_{\mathrm{obs}})$-measurable functions) composed with the Doob $L^2$ isomorphism doobL2Equiv. Downstream theorems (lem:25.34, thm:25.40, lem:25.41, cor:25.42) reference the operator and the Doob equivalence's defining identity doobL2Equiv_comp_apply by name.

    Bibliographic comments. The systematic theory of observed-data scores under coarsening, and the Coarsening At Random (CAR) condition that lets the observed score be obtained as a conditional expectation of the full-data score, originates with R. D. Gill, M. J. van der Laan, and J. M. Robins, "Coarsening at random: characterizations, conjectures, counter-examples," in Proceedings of the First Seattle Symposium in Biostatistics: Survival Analysis (D. Y. Lin and T. R. Fleming, eds.), Lecture Notes in Statistics 123, Springer, 1997, pp. 255–294. That work generalizes the missing-at-random (MAR) notion of D. B. Rubin, "Inference and missing data," Biometrika 63 (1976), 581–592, to general coarsening mechanisms and characterizes when the coarsening is ancillary for the full-data parameter. Van der Vaart's §25.5.2 (Information Loss Models) presents the resulting information-loss operator as a Hilbert-space projection; the conditional-expectation form formalized here synthesizes that line of work.

      The information-loss operator on the mean-zero subspaces #

      The raw operator already lands a.e. in the mean-zero subspace (conditional expectation preserves the integral and the Doob isometry is measure-preserving), so composing it with the orthogonal projection onto L²₀(P_full.map M) is the identity correction: it does not change the value, but it lets Lean see the codomain membership via the Doob behavioral identity doobL2Equiv_comp_apply. The clean identity ↑(Π s) = doobL2Equiv (condExpL2 … s) is recorded separately in informationLossOperator_coe_eq.
