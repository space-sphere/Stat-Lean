    QMD limit for the coarsened path (vdV Lem 25.34-I, the qmd_limit field) #

    This file proves the score-preservation qmd_limit field of QMDPath.coarsen. Van der Vaart states this result as Lemma 25.34 half I (book p.375) and refers its proof to [139, pp.188-193]. The headline coarsen_qmd_limit is the exact field statement; Operators/CoarsenedQMD.lean plugs it into the structure.

    The denominator-safe conditional-projection argument proceeds by writing p̄ t := (dQ_t/dμ).toReal, ξ t := √(p̄ t), q̃ t := μ[p̄ t | comap M], cross t := μ[ξt·ξ0 | comap M], A t := cross t / √q̃0 (guarded), the pulled-back field residual splits as RES = [A − √q̃0 − (t/2)(Π∘M)√q̃0] + [√q̃t − A], whose two brackets are each o(t) in L²(μ) (the first by conditional Cauchy–Schwarz on the QMD remainder, the second by a ρ-ratio DCT-along-subsequence argument).

    Internal definitions pbar, xi, qtilde, cross, Cdom, Acoef, and rho package the notation.

    Headline declaration: coarsen_qmd_limit.
