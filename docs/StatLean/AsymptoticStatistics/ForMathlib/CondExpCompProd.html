    Conditional expectation under a composition-product measure (Doob-fibre form) #

    For a probability measure Q : Measure 𝓨, a Markov kernel r : Kernel 𝓨 𝓓, and the composition-product Q ⊗ₘ r on 𝓨 × 𝓓, the conditional expectation of an integrable f : 𝓨 × 𝓓 → ℝ under the σ-algebra generated by the first coordinate Prod.fst is the fibrewise kernel average p ↦ ∫ δ, f (p.1, δ) ∂(r p.1).

    This is the compProd-specialization of the abstract characterization MeasureTheory.ae_eq_condExp_of_forall_setIntegral_eq: the candidate p ↦ ∫ δ, f (p.1, δ) ∂(r p.1) is comap Prod.fst-measurable and matches f on every comap Prod.fst-measurable rectangle by Measure.setIntegral_compProd, so it is a version of the conditional expectation.

    It is the measure-theoretic bridge feeding the CAR coarsening-score identification (Operators/CoarseningScoreIdentification.lean, vdV §25.5.3 thm:25.40): the tower E_{Q,R}(b(X) | Y = y) = ∫ b(M(y, δ)) r(δ | y) dν(δ) is this lemma applied to f = b ∘ M.

    Conditional expectation under Q ⊗ₘ r given the first coordinate is the fibrewise kernel average.

    For an integrable f : 𝓨 × 𝓓 → ℝ, the conditional expectation of f under the sub-σ-algebra comap Prod.fst generated by the 𝓨-coordinate equals, Q ⊗ₘ r-a.e., the fibre integral p ↦ ∫ δ, f (p.1, δ) ∂(r p.1).

    The candidate is comap Prod.fst-measurable (a function of p.1) and integrable; on a rectangle Prod.fst ⁻¹' s = s ×ˢ univ with s measurable in 𝓨, Measure.setIntegral_compProd gives ∫_{s×univ} f d(Q ⊗ₘ r) = ∫_s (∫ f(y, ·) d(r y)) dQ, so the candidate satisfies the defining set-integral identity; conclude with ae_eq_condExp_of_forall_setIntegral_eq.
