    Convergence in probability from mean + variance control.

    Standalone probability lemma used in Step 4 of Theorem 7.2: if E[Y_n] → c and Var[Y_n] → 0, then Y_n → c in probability.

    Theorem-agnostic; lives outside any chapter directory.
