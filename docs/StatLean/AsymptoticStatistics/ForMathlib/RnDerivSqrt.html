    Square-root density helpers for absolutely continuous measures.

    When ν ≪ μ with μ σ-finite, the Radon–Nikodym derivative p := ν.rnDeriv μ is μ-a.e. finite, and √p ∈ L²(μ) with ‖√p‖²_{L²(μ)} = ν(univ). The bridge between L²(μ)-membership of g · √p and L²(ν)-membership of g, plus the integral identity ∫ g dν = ∫ g · p dμ, is what Core/QMDPath.score_in_L2ZeroMean needs to lift its bare-function score to ↥(L2ZeroMean P).

    These theorem-agnostic lemmas belong to the ForMathlib layer.

    For a finite measure ν ≪ μ with μ σ-finite, the square-root density √(dν/dμ) is in L²(μ). The norm is computed in integral_sqrt_rnDeriv_sq.

    Hellinger-type identity: the squared L²(μ)-norm of √(dν/dμ) equals ν(univ). For ν a probability measure this reduces to 1.

    theorem AsymptoticStatistics.ForMathlib.RnDerivSqrt.integral_eq_integral_mul_rnDeriv_of_ac {Ω : Type u_1} [MeasurableSpace Ω] {P μ : MeasureTheory.Measure Ω} [MeasureTheory.IsFiniteMeasure P] [MeasureTheory.SigmaFinite μ] (h_ac : P.AbsolutelyContinuous μ) (g : Ω → ℝ) :
    ∫ (ω : Ω), g ω ∂P = ∫ (ω : Ω), g ω * (P.rnDeriv μ ω).toReal ∂μ

    Integral identity: ∫ g dP = ∫ g · (dP/dμ) dμ for P ≪ μ with μ σ-finite. The integrability of g under P is not needed for the equality (both sides are defined as Bochner integrals, which fall back to 0 when the integrand fails integrability).
