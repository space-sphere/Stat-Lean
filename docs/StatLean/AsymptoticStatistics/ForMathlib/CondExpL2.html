    Doob-factorization helpers at the Lp level #

    When f : α → β is measurable, every (σ_β.comap f)-measurable function on α factors as g ∘ f for some measurable g : β → ℝ (Doob's factorization lemma). At the L^p level this gives an isometric isomorphism between the (σ_β.comap f)-measurable subspace of Lp ℝ p μ and Lp ℝ p (μ.map f).

    This file packages the Doob L² isomorphism (doobL2Equiv) plus its key behavioral lemma as named declarations. The mathematical content is standard (Bogachev II §10.4; Kallenberg, Theorem 6.4).
