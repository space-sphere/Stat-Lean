    Vector deterministic-Slutsky / recentering bridge #

    For the k-extension of semiparametric_convolution_theorem (productMeasure_unbounded_pushforward_vec_target), we need to swap the parametric recentering term ψ(curve(1/√n)) for the affine approximation ψ_proj_vec ((√n)⁻¹ • h). The two differ by o((√n)⁻¹) deterministically (pathwise differentiability + first-order Taylor on the affine functional), so vector weak convergence of the recentered statistic is preserved across the swap via a deterministic-shift Slutsky argument.

    Vector deterministic-Slutsky / recentering bridge.

    If (P n).map (fun ω => Xn n ω + cn n) ⇝ L weakly on E and the deterministic shift cn → 0, then (P n).map (Xn n) ⇝ L as well.

    Used in productMeasure_unbounded_pushforward_vec_target to swap the parametric recentering ψ(curve(1/√n)) for the affine approximation ψ_proj_vec ((√n)⁻¹ • h): the deterministic difference vanishes by pathwise differentiability of ψ plus first-order Taylor on the affine ψ_proj_vec.

    Proof idea: composition of WeakConverges.map for the continuous shift functor with the limit identification under Tendsto cn 0.
