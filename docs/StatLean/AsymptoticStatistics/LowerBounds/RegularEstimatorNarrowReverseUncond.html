    Unconditional reverse direction IsRegularEstimator_narrow ⟹ IsRegularEstimator #

    RegularEstimatorNarrowReverse.isRegularEstimator_of_narrow_explicit proves the reverse direction only for realising paths with dominating = P (it carries h_dom_γ and h_chosen_dom). With the common-dominator LAN-locality hellinger_locality_for_qmdpath_same_score_common_dom, those side conditions are removed: for an arbitrary realising γ and chosen γ₀ we transfer along the common dominator ξ = γ.dominating + γ₀.dominating (each ≪ ξ), so narrow regularity implies the full all-paths IsRegularEstimator with no dominator restriction.

    Dom-free reverse direction (explicit chosen-family form).

    For an arbitrary realising γ : QMDPath P with score g, narrow regularity along a chosen family γ₀ := chosenFamily g hg (with score g) yields the recentered-at-perturbed-truth weak convergence — with no dominating = P restriction on either γ or γ₀. The Hellinger-locality transfer (Step A) runs through the common dominator ξ = γ.dominating + γ₀.dominating; Steps B (ψ-shift Slutsky) and C (chosen-family convergence) are dominator-free.
