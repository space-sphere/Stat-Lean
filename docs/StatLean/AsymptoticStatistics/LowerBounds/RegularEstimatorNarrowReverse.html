    Reverse direction of the narrow/broad regularity equivalence #

    The non-trivial direction of IsRegularEstimator_narrow ⟺ IsRegularEstimator:

    IsRegularEstimator_narrow P T_set ψ hψ hEIF T_n L ⟹ IsRegularEstimator P T_set ψ hψ hEIF T_n L (restricted to natural QMDPaths)

    Under a dominator-scope restriction (both the chosen family and the arbitrary realising QMDPath γ have dominating := P), narrow regularity implies broad regularity. Reference: vdV §25.3.2 (paragraph preceding Theorem 25.20) — Hájek-style regularity at a chosen submodel transfers to any same-score submodel.

    Headline declarations: isRegularEstimator_of_narrow_explicit and its packaged wrapper isRegularEstimator_of_narrow.

    Proof structure #

    Let γ (arbitrary) and γ₀ := chosenFamily g hg both have score g and dominator P. Goal: weak convergence of the γ-perturbed rescaled estimator to L. Per test function f ∈ BCF:

    • (A) |∫ (f ∘ Fγ) d((γ.curve(t_n))^n) - ∫ (f ∘ Fγ) d((γ₀.curve(t_n))^n)| → 0 by hellinger_locality_for_qmdpath_same_score + the wrapper ForMathlib.HellingerIntegralBound.integral_test_diff_tendsto_zero.
    • (B) |∫ (f ∘ Fγ) d((γ₀.curve(t_n))^n) - ∫ (f ∘ Fγ₀) d((γ₀.curve(t_n))^n)| → 0 via ψ-shift Slutsky (deterministic shift → 0 by hψ.derivative_spec).
    • (C) ∫ (f ∘ Fγ₀) d((γ₀.curve(t_n))^n) → ∫ f dL from h_chosen_regular.

    Triangle inequality assembles (A) + (B) + (C).

    Reverse direction of the narrow/broad equivalence (explicit chosen-family form).

    Under a dominator-scope restriction (both the chosen family and the arbitrary realising QMDPath have dominating := P), narrow regularity implies broad regularity.

    Hypotheses #

    • chosenFamily g hg: a QMDPath P realising score g for each g ∈ tangentSpace T_set.
    • h_chosen_dom: every chosen-family entry has dominating := P.
    • h_chosen_score: scores match.
    • h_chosen_regular: the weak convergence holds along the chosen family.
    • hT_meas: the estimator sequence is measurable.

    Conclusion #

    For every realising γ : QMDPath P with dominating := P, the rescaled estimator computed along γ and recentered at the perturbed truth converges weakly to L.

    Reference: vdV §25.3.2 (paragraph preceding Theorem 25.20) — Hájek-style regularity at a chosen submodel transfers to any same-score submodel because Hellinger LAN-locality implies TV-zero between the corresponding product measures, and TV-zero implies bounded-functional-integral equality (and hence weak-limit equality).
