    DQM expansions in square-root density-ratio coordinates #

    Named deterministic cores for the population Taylor expansion in van der Vaart, Theorem 5.39. They expose the L² linearization, mean, second moment, and uniform-integrability tails of Wθ = 2(√(pθ/p₀)-1).

    DQM makes the square-root density ratio L²-linear with derivative x ↦ ⟨θ-θ₀, ℓ(x)⟩ under the true law.
