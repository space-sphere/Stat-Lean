    Maximum-likelihood consistency from KL identifiability #

    This file combines vdV Lemma 5.35 with Theorem 5.7. Following the technically advantageous route on book p.63, the real-valued criterion is the stabilized mixture log-likelihood

    log ((p_theta + p_theta0) / (2 * p_theta0)).

    The KL lemma derives strict uniqueness, the compact-superlevel adapter derives the strong separation required by Theorem 5.7, and mEstimator_consistent performs the stochastic assembly. No consistency or separation conclusion is accepted as a hypothesis.
