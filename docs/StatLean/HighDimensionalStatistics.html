    HighDimensionalStatistics — area umbrella #

    OLS mean-squared-error, Lasso statistical rates, and compressed sensing, formalized from Lu, Big Data Analysis, ch. 5, 6, 7 and 8. Consumes the ConcentrationInequalities sub-Gaussian / sub-exponential / covering bricks and the AsymptoticStatistics Gaussian-MGF bricks for the noise and random-matrix bounds. Compressed sensing (ch. 6–7): the cone theorem (thm:cone), perfect recovery under RIP (thm:rip), and the random-Gaussian RIP guarantee (thm:3s-rip). Modules are imported above as each lands.
